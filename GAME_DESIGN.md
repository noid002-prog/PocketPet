# Pocket Pet v0.4 – Game Design Snapshot

Core loop: **Care → Interact → Play → Optional Adventure/Battle → Grow**.

The home screen remains the center of the game. Most interactions happen directly on the pet rather than through complex menus.

## Growth
Egg → Pichu → Pikachu → Raichu → optional special Mega form (internal fan prototype). Growth depends on attention, level and bond rather than battle alone.

## Direct interaction
- Tap: head pat
- Swipe: pet/stroke
- Hold: cuddle
- Food: drag chosen food onto pet
- Ball: drag/throw and pet chases it
- Bath: 3 gentle swipes with bubbles
- Sleep: short nap animation
- Idle: pet slowly wanders around the room

## Child-safety rules
No pet death, no public chat, no strangers, no ads, no purchases/loot boxes, no punishment for long absence, no level/item loss on battle defeat. Needs decay is capped; after a long absence the pet is treated as safely cared for.

## Playtest progression
Fast thresholds are used so testers can experience the whole lifecycle in a short session. Final balancing should stretch growth across multiple relaxed sessions and avoid pressure-based retention mechanics.
